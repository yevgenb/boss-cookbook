## Ingredients

- Broccoli florets
- Olive oil
- Salt + pepper
- Lemon (zest + juice)
- Optional: parmesan, chili flakes, garlic

## Steps

1. Heat oven to 425°F / 220°C.
2. Toss broccoli with olive oil, salt, pepper.
3. Roast 18–25 min, flipping once, until browned.
4. Finish with lemon zest + juice. Add parmesan if you want.
