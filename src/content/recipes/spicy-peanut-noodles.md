## Ingredients

- Noodles (ramen, udon, or spaghetti)
- Peanut butter
- Soy sauce
- Rice vinegar or lime juice
- Chili crisp / chili oil
- Garlic (minced)
- Optional: sesame oil, scallions, cucumber, crushed peanuts

## Steps

1. Cook noodles. Reserve a splash of noodle water.
2. Whisk sauce: peanut butter + soy + vinegar/lime + chili crisp + garlic (+ sesame oil).
3. Toss noodles with sauce, loosen with noodle water.
4. Top with scallions / cucumber / peanuts.

## Notes

Adjust heat with chili crisp; adjust salt with soy; adjust brightness with lime.
